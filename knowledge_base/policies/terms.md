# Terms of Service and Usage Guidelines

## Community Standards
The platform is a professional networking and educational environment. By joining, users agree to maintain a high standard of professional conduct. Harassment, spamming, or inappropriate behavior during mentor sessions or in community forums will result in immediate account suspension.

## Automated Access and Scraping
To protect the privacy of our students and the integrity of our Alumni Talent Pool, unauthorized automated data collection (scraping) is strictly prohibited. External partners or recruiters who wish to access the talent pool must do so through official, verified channels and partnerships established with the platform administrators.
