# Privacy and Data Protection

## Protecting Personal Information
The platform operates on a strict privacy-first model. We understand that users share sensitive academic and career information with us, and we protect it rigorously. Personal Identifiable Information (PII) is isolated and kept strictly confidential.

## How We Use Data
The data provided by users—such as their interests, grades, and career goals—is used exclusively to improve their experience. It powers the AI matching engines that recommend mentors, jobs, and scholarships. We do not sell user data to third-party marketing agencies.

## AI and Privacy
When our Artificial Intelligence engines analyze user data to provide recommendations, all direct identifiers (like names and contact info) are stripped away. The AI only looks at the underlying skills and goals, ensuring that privacy is maintained even when utilizing advanced machine learning models.
