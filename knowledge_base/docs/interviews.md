# Career AI and Mock Interviews

## Practice Makes Perfect
Interviewing is a skill, and the platform provides an AI-powered environment to practice. Students can engage in realistic mock interviews directly within the platform. They can choose to practice general behavioral questions or dive deep into highly technical assessments tailored to their specific industry.

## Real-Time Evaluation
During a mock interview, the AI carefully analyzes the student's responses. It evaluates their answers based on clarity, confidence, and technical accuracy. At the end of the session, the student receives a detailed breakdown of how they performed, highlighting what they did well and where they need to improve.

## Credential Badges
If a student performs exceptionally well during a technical AI interview, the platform can automatically award them a credential badge. These badges are displayed publicly on their profile, serving as verified proof of their skills to prospective mentors and employers.
