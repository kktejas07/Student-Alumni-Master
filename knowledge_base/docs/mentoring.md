# AI-Powered Mentorship System

## Connecting Students and Professionals
The mentoring system bridges the gap between students and experienced alumni. Mentors define their available times on a calendar. Students can browse mentor profiles, view their expertise, and request to book a time slot. Mentoring sessions can be booked for free if the mentor provides it. Once a session is booked, the platform handles the scheduling and reminders.

## AI Mentor Matching
Finding the right mentor can be daunting, so the platform uses Artificial Intelligence to do the heavy lifting. The AI analyzes a student's career aspirations, current skills, and academic background, and cross-references this with the entire database of mentors. It then recommends the best possible matches, explaining exactly why a particular mentor is a good fit.

## Smart Session Preparation
To ensure mentorship sessions are highly productive, the AI acts as a copilot for the mentor. Before a scheduled call, the system generates a detailed briefing document for the mentor. This brief highlights the student's recent activities, identifies potential skill gaps, and suggests discussion topics, ensuring the mentor is fully prepared to help the student succeed.

## Feedback and Ratings
After a session concludes, students can leave a rating and review for their mentor. Mentors can also reply to these reviews. High ratings improve a mentor's visibility and platform reputation.
