# Platform Onboarding Guide

## Introduction
The onboarding process is the first step for any user joining the platform. The system is designed to support multiple types of users, each with a unique journey: Students, Alumni, Mentors, and College Administrators.

## Student Onboarding
When a student signs up, they provide essential academic details such as their institution name, their current year of study, and their major or stream. Crucially, students also input their career goals, areas of interest, and current academic standing (like CGPA). This information is instantly used by the platform to personalize their dashboard, recommending highly relevant jobs, courses, and events right from day one.

## Alumni and Mentor Onboarding
Alumni and professionals joining the platform provide their graduation details, current employment status, and industry expertise. During this process, alumni can opt into the Talent Pool, making them visible to top recruiters. Furthermore, they can choose to become mentors. If they opt into mentoring, they provide detailed information about the subjects they can teach, their years of experience, and their availability schedule. They can even set their pricing for professional mentorship sessions.

## Profile Completion
As part of the process, users can upload a profile photo and fill out a biography. Once these steps are finished, their profile is marked as complete, unlocking the full potential of the platform's networking and career tools.
