# Student Hub and Features

## The Central Dashboard
The student dashboard is the central hub for academic and career advancement. When a student logs in, they are presented with a personalized feed of upcoming campus events, recent job postings matching their major, and recommended mentors who work in their desired industry.

## Activity Credits and Wallet
To encourage active participation, the platform uses a gamified credit system. Students earn activity credits by logging in regularly, completing educational courses, reaching milestones on their career roadmap, and attending events. These credits are stored in a digital wallet and can be redeemed for various rewards, such as free mentor sessions or premium platform features.

## Personalized Intelligence
The platform constantly analyzes the student's activities and provides AI-driven insights. For example, if a student is aiming for a career in software engineering but lacks Python skills, the system will actively highlight this gap and recommend specific actions to improve.

## Learning and Courses
Students have access to a comprehensive course marketplace. The platform aggregates high-quality educational content from top providers (like university open courseware and coding bootcamps). Students can enroll in these tracks, monitor their progress, and receive AI recommendations on which courses to take next to achieve their dream job.
