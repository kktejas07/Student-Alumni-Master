# Personalized Job and Internship Feed

## Aggregating the Best Opportunities
The job feed is the ultimate career tool. Instead of relying on a single source, the platform actively aggregates full-time jobs, part-time roles, and internships from multiple major external job boards across the web. This ensures the feed is always populated with the freshest and most diverse opportunities.

## Smart Filtering and Ranking
The system does not just show a raw list of jobs; it personalizes the feed for every single user. Based on a student's graduation year, their platform tier, and their stated career goals, the platform filters out irrelevant roles. A freshman will see applicable internships, while a graduating senior will see entry-level full-time roles in their chosen industry.

## Tracking and Analytics
Students can save jobs they are interested in and track the roles they have applied for. The platform also analyzes application trends across the entire student body to highlight "Trending Companies," showing users where their peers are most successfully finding employment.
