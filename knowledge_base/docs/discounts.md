# Student Discounts and Deals

## Exclusive Perks
Being a verified student comes with perks. The Deals Aggregator is a dedicated section of the platform that pulls together exclusive discounts on software, lifestyle products, travel, and educational tools. 

## Curation and Savings
The platform continuously hunts for the best student deals (such as the GitHub Student Developer Pack) and calculates exactly how much money a student can save by claiming them. The deals are categorized neatly, and the platform often curates the top offers of the month, ensuring students get the maximum value out of their verified academic status.
