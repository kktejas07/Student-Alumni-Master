# Alumni Network and Talent Pool

## The Alumni Experience
The Alumni module is designed to help graduates give back to their community while also advancing their own careers. The platform allows alumni to maintain a professional profile showcasing their current role, industry expertise, and career history.

## The Talent Pool
A major feature for alumni is the Talent Pool. By opting into the Talent Pool, alumni make their profiles visible to verified external recruiters and corporate partners. The system allows recruiters to search the talent pool based on specific skills, location, and years of experience, acting as an exclusive recruitment pipeline.

## Mentorship and Giving Back
Alumni are the backbone of the platform's mentoring program. Graduates can seamlessly convert their standard account into a mentor account. Once active, they can set their availability, define the topics they are comfortable coaching on, and start accepting session requests from current students. The platform tracks how many students they have helped and awards them reputation badges for their contributions.
