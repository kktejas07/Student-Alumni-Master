# Campus and Global Events

## Event Discovery
The platform acts as a central calendar for both local college events and massive global opportunities. Students can discover a wide variety of events, including campus career fairs, departmental webinars, and external tech hackathons.

## Automated Aggregation
To ensure students never miss an opportunity, the platform automatically scours the internet for high-quality external events. It connects to major global hackathon and tech event directories, pulls in the details, and neatly formats them into the platform's calendar. The system is smart enough to detect and remove duplicate events.

## RSVPs and Calendar Sync
Registering for an event is a one-click process. When a user RSVPs, their spot is reserved, and the event is added to their internal platform schedule. Users can also export the event details to their personal Google or Apple calendars. The platform ensures students get timely reminders before an event begins.
