# Frequently Asked Questions (Student Perspective)

**Q: How do I earn Activity Credits and what can I use them for?**
A: You earn credits by actively participating on the platform—such as logging in, completing courses, hitting milestones on your roadmap, and attending events. These are tracked securely in the background. You can redeem these credits for rewards, exclusive mentor sessions, or view your wallet balance on your dashboard.

**Q: How does the AI match me with the right mentor?**
A: Our Mentor AI system analyzes your profile, including your career interests, education level, and goals, and maps it against the expertise of available alumni. It identifies your current skill gaps and even generates a personalized prep guide for your mentor before the session, so you get the most out of your time.

**Q: Where do the jobs and internships in my feed come from?**
A: Your job feed is highly personalized and aggregated from multiple major external boards (like RemoteOK, The Muse, and Arbeitnow). The platform filters and ranks these jobs based on your current year of study, your platform tier, and your specific career goals, ensuring you only see roles you qualify for.

**Q: Can I practice for interviews on the platform?**
A: Yes! You can use the Career AI feature to run mock interviews. The AI chat interface will evaluate your responses for clarity, technical accuracy, and confidence. Performing well in these AI assessments can earn you credential badges that are displayed on your profile to employers.

**Q: How do I track my learning and workshop progress?**
A: Once you enroll in a course or workshop (which includes curated, high-quality content synced from providers like MIT OCW or freeCodeCamp), your progress is tracked automatically. The AI advisor can also recommend specific learning tracks to help you bridge any skill gaps.

**Q: How is my platform Tier or Trust Score calculated?**
A: Your tier is continuously updated by our achievement system. It evaluates your active engagement over the last 30 days, courses completed, events attended, and successful mentor connections. High engagement unlocks better visibility and higher-tier job postings.

**Q: Does the platform help me find scholarships or funding?**
A: Absolutely. The financial services portal aggregates scholarships, student loans, and startup grants. Our Financial AI analyzes your CGPA, demographics, and college information to generate a list of highly personalized funding matches, explaining exactly why you are a strong candidate for each one.

**Q: How do I access student discounts and perks?**
A: When you onboard using your official college details, you gain access to the Deals Aggregator. This unlocks curated student-only discounts (like software developer packs, lifestyle perks, and travel) and explicitly shows your total savings.
